🦀 Pinch Points 🦀 a crab-routing video game

One to six players, on the couch or over the wire.

Download free View the source

Linux · macOS · Windows · open source

Arrows are your only verb

Every crab crossing one turns that way. Three on the sand at a time, and they wash away.

The gulls are steerable

Point one at a rival's castle and it leaves with half their score. That is the whole offensive game.

The tide is the clock

The sea closes in around the board. Last thirty seconds, the gulls double.

What's in the box

  • Tide Pool 100 solo puzzles

  • Driftwood level editor
  • Beach Day score attack
  • Daily Challenge same for everyone
  • Replays shareable as text

Grab it

Paste one line into a terminal. It fetches the latest release for your machine.

macOS · Linux

curl -fsSL https://agourlay.github.io/pinch-points/install.sh | sh

Installs to ~/.local/bin · Read the script

Windows (PowerShell)

irm https://agourlay.github.io/pinch-points/install.ps1 | iex

Installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs and adds it to your PATH · Read the script

Or download it yourself

With Rust installed: cargo install --locked pinch-points
