Arrows are your only verb

Every crab crossing one turns that way. Three on the sand at a time, and they wash away.

The gulls are steerable

Point one at a rival's castle and it leaves with half their score. That is the whole offensive game.

The tide is the clock

The sea closes in around the board. Last thirty seconds, the gulls double.
